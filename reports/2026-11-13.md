## Papers

**SALA: Semantically Aligned Latent Actions Enable Dual-Axis Scaling of Embodied Foundation Models**

Latent action models let human video supervise robot policies, but learning latents from video reconstruction alone clusters them by visual appearance rather than by the action itself, so human and robot actions never line up. SALA fixes the alignment with language: it uses descriptions of unpaired human and robot clips to estimate semantic similarities, then guides human latents toward semantically related robot latents while robot action reconstruction grounds the space in control. The payoff is scaling on two axes — more general human video in pretraining steadily cuts robot action prediction error, and more task-specific human video substantially lifts real-world task success.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17110)

**STVLA: Spatial-aware and Temporal-memory for Vision-Language-Action Models**

Most VLA policies act on the current observation alone, forgetting both the scene's 3D structure and how it changed during the task. STVLA folds features from a frozen visual geometry model into a bounded temporal memory alongside visual tokens, and retrieves history with a scoring rule that balances relevance to the current observation against complementary evidence. It tops every compared method on LIBERO, LIBERO-Mem, MIKASA-Robo, and SimplerEnv Bridge, and on a real AGIBOT G2 it beats π0.5 by 58, 30, and 35 percentage points on block stacking, object organization, and phone packaging.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16589) · [Project page](https://stvla.github.io/)

**FORGE: Force-Aware Multi-Granularity Control for Efficient Humanoid Loco-Manipulation**

Humanoid loco-manipulation needs precision, balance, and contact awareness at once, and teleoperating demonstrations for it is hard. FORGE is a whole-body controller with a coarse-to-fine command space — base velocity, foot placement, wrist poses, optional desired wrist loads — trained without full-body human motion references, on sparse wrist poses alone. For sustained contact it estimates wrist interaction forces from proprioception alone, no force/torque sensors, via a momentum observer plus a learned correction. It runs at 22 ms latency with lower tracking error than prior controllers, and as a diffusion-policy executor it completes a squat-and-lift task at 100% unloaded and 90% with a 2 kg payload.

**EgoX: Egocentric Cross-Embodiment Manipulation with Embodiment Dreaming**

Human, robot, and simulated demonstrations could all teach one generalist policy, but their morphologies, sensors, and control interfaces don't line up. EgoX organizes them through shared egocentric observation and action modalities and pretrains MXT+ — embodiment-specific tokenizers and action experts around a shared Transformer trunk — with a training-only "embodiment dreaming" objective that predicts future embodiment latents. Across 13 task–embodiment pairs spanning five tasks and six embodiments, cross-embodiment pretraining lifts average task progress from 0.63 to 0.80 over training from scratch, and from 0.53 to 0.77 under out-of-distribution conditions.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16728) · [Project page](https://egox-dream.github.io/)

**Reins: Frontier Multimodal Models Driving Real Robot Arms on Long-Horizon Tasks, Zero-Shot**

Frontier models can plan and call tools, but driving a real arm through long, contact-rich manipulation usually defeats them — they are blind while the arm moves and while they think. Reins is a harness that lets an off-the-shelf model operate a dual-arm robot with 3D perception tools and atomic actions that report contact and grip; actions chain in one call, each with a condition that must hold, while a second "narrator" instance watches the cameras continuously, and a simulation-ready twin of the scene is built from images for continual learning. With natural-language prompts and no task-specific code, GPT-6.1 Sol succeeds in 88% of real episodes across five tasks; without the harness it fails every shell game and takes 8× as long.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16632)

## Hot Discussions

**Skild AI open-sources Steel, its physics solver, on X**

Skild AI announced it is open-sourcing Steel, its internal physics solver built with NVIDIA, claiming simulations up to 13× faster than PhysX while using about 3× less GPU memory — pitched as unlocking scalable simulation for manipulation for everyone. The post, from November 9, had drawn over 111K views and 1,297 likes when checked.

[View post](https://x.com/SkildAI/status/2119844954893946999)

**Gemini Robotics 2.5 reaches the factory floor**

Google DeepMind introduced Gemini Robotics 2.5 on November 10, calling it its most capable vision-language-action model to date, evaluated across more than 200 tasks with 30–100% higher success rates than its predecessor. The headline demo: Apptronik's Apollo 2 humanoid sorting automotive parts on live Mercedes-Benz production lines at 96% success. DeepMind also released ASIMOV-Jailbreak, an open benchmark of adversarial prompts for robot safety. All figures are company-reported; the model is rolling out to robotics partners only, with no public API yet.

[Announcement](https://deepmind.google/blog/gemini-robotics-2-5-accelerates-real-world-physical-intelligence/) · [View post](https://www.threads.com/@google/post/Dfm4cpjkrpa)
