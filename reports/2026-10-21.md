## Papers

**PLaCA: Learning Patch-Level Future Latent Change for Action Generation**

A world-to-action VLA that predicts patch-level future latent change — how each image region is expected to change — instead of full future images, and feeds it into action generation through a gated future-to-action interface. Hits 79.0% on LIBERO-Long and lifts CALVIN average sequence length from 2.784 to 3.564 while updating only 2.95% of parameters in the second training stage.


[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27657)

**SuperMimic: Scaling Humanoid Whole-Body Control with One Differentiable World Model**

Trains a humanoid motion-tracking policy together with one differentiable world model of shared dynamics, using backprop-through-time over imagined rollouts instead of massive model-free RL. Surpasses a state-of-the-art baseline (7 days on 128 GPUs) in 3 hours on a single GPU — a 1000× reduction in GPU-hours — and transfers to a physical Unitree G1 across diverse and acrobatic motions. From Pieter Abbeel's group, removing compute as the bottleneck for humanoid whole-body control scaling.


[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28480)

**De-Diffusing WAMs: Action-Sufficient Direct Prediction for Robot Control**

Converts diffusion-pretrained cascade world action models into single-step future/action predictors via action-sufficient distillation — training the future predictor by backpropagating action error through the expert rather than pixel matching. DirectWAM reaches 97.1% average success across four LIBERO suites and 85.4% on RoboTwin 2.0 with 28× lower inference latency, a practical route to real-time WAMs.


[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28122)

**Training Controllable World Models via Action Influence Maximization**

Diagnoses a core failure mode of action-conditioned video world models: training on narrow expert demonstrations teaches them to ignore the commanded action and render plausible futures regardless. Proves dataset "action influence" upper-bounds what any trained model can express, then introduces AIM active data collection that matches a 300k-episode model with just 3k demos + 500 collected episodes — an 85× data reduction.


[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27387)

**Physical Canvas: A Vision Tactile World Model For Robot Policy Evaluation And Learning**

PhysCanvas unifies multi-view RGB, tactile images, marker displacement, task state, and actions in one vision space so a world model can serve as a faithful interactive simulator for contact-rich manipulation. State Reward Model supervision raises world-model-to-real success correlation from 0.602 to 0.885, and in-world-model post-training lifts π₀.₅ real-world success from 59.1% to 70.8%.


[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28613)

## Hot Discussions

**Eka Robotics raises $150M Series A at $1.45B for its Vision-Force-Action model**

The stealth-exit story of the day: the Pulkit Agrawal / Tuomas Haarnoja startup trains a VFA model on physics simulation and large-scale RL rather than human video, claiming 1,500 picks/hour at 95%+ success with a sub-$1,000 force-sensing gripper. Round co-led by a16z and F-Prime; commentators are calling it the anti-VLA play — mastering physics instead of mimicking humans.

**Kyutai releases REKI, a 2.4B open-weights VLA with efficient memory**

A small set of register tokens in the action expert summarizes each frame's VLM output and persists in the expert's cache, giving the policy cheap access to its recent past — top score on LIBERO-Plus, 4× fewer pose oscillations, 21.8 ms inference on one H100. Full code and weights released, pretrained only on public data, sparking active community discussion about the new size–performance Pareto frontier for VLAs.
