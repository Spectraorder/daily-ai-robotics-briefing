## Hot Discussions

**Embo exits stealth with a $110M seed to build world-model robot brains**

Danijar Hafner — the former DeepMind researcher behind the Dreamer line of world-model agents — has launched Embo, a startup building one general-purpose world model as the brain for humanoid robots, backed by a $110M seed round led by a16z with Khosla Ventures, DST Global, and NVIDIA's NVentures. The plan: pretrain on internet-scale video, fine-tune on data from its own humanoid fleet, then let robots practice skills inside the learned world model with reinforcement learning. No product, valuation, or benchmarks yet — but the team draws from DeepMind, Meta Superintelligence Labs, NVIDIA Cosmos, UC Berkeley, and MIT.

[View post](https://www.threads.com/@marcus_sanfrancisco/post/DgS2BFhCKbP) · [Dealroom report](https://dealroom.co/news/175093-embo-raises-110m-seed-to-build-robot-brains-with-world-models/)

**ShengShu ships Motubrain 2.0, a 20B world-action model with a harness**

ShengShu Technology, the company behind the Vidu video model, has released Motubrain 2.0, a 20-billion-parameter world-action model paired with Motubrain-Harness, an agent layer for planning, execution, and failure recovery. The model jointly predicts future video and actions from multi-view input, trained on a reported 2.5M hours of data, and the company claims top scores on four of six embodied benchmarks — all self-reported, with weights marked "coming next week," so independent checks will have to wait.

[View post](https://www.threads.com/@swapnil.ricksarkar/post/DgSSwdMmC1X) · [Official page](https://www.genspi.com/en/motubrain/)

**Xiaomi Auto releases World Model 2.0 for driving and robots**

Xiaomi Auto has released World Model 2.0, a unified streaming causal world model covering multi-view video generation, physical interaction, 3D reconstruction (WorldRec 2.0), and action modeling, trained on a reported 12.6M clips across 1–7 camera views. It is already deployed internally for synthetic data and closed-loop simulation testing, where a policy trained in its simulator reports a 0.4% collision rate across 1,000 scenarios; the underlying paper dates from November 1, but the release itself is new this week.

[View post](https://www.instagram.com/p/DgPYmmWyai7/) · [Project page](https://xiaomiautoworldmodel.github.io/CausalWorldModel/) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01677)
