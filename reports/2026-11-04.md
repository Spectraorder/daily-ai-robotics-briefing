# Daily AI Robotics Briefing — November 4, 2026

Wednesday's edition is about learning without robot demos: a VLM that sketches a minimal world model instead of outputting actions, a virtual try-on trick that turns human videos into robot demonstrations, an open harness that lets an open-weight model outscore a frontier agent on RoboDojo, and a perception-first foundation model for physical AI — plus Liber-0's world-action launch, handheld-demo robot hands, and a warning that VLAs do not know when not to act.

## Papers

**WorldSketch: Sketching World Models for Zero-Shot Mobile Manipulation**

Instead of asking a VLM to pick a skill or write motion code, WorldSketch gives it a third job: sketch a minimal kinematic world model — only the task-relevant objects, their interaction geometry, and possible motions — which a geometric planner then turns into contact and whole-body motion, revising the sketch when execution contradicts it. Zero-shot, it reaches 91% on RoboCasa, 97% on LIBERO-PRO, and 76% on R2R-CE navigation, while using far fewer VLM tokens than step-by-step agents.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00513)

**RoboTryOn: Synthesizing Dexterous Robot Demonstrations from Human Videos via Virtual Try-On**

Human videos are plentiful but show the wrong embodiment. RoboTryOn treats human-to-robot transfer as virtual try-on: keep the demonstrated motion, swap the human for the target robot — translating keyframes autoregressively, synthesizing motion guided by the human hand skeleton, then labeling actions with a depth-aware inverse dynamics model. Policies trained on the synthetic data jump from 2% to 60% average success on novel tasks, including bimanual manipulation, with no real robot demonstrations of those tasks, matching teleoperation-trained policies at roughly a third of the collection cost.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05163)

**Democratizing Robot-Use Agents: A Unified Harness with Open Models**

Robot-use agents have been gated behind proprietary frontier models. uni-robot-agent is a unified, modular harness — shared tools, feedback formats, and environment adapters across simulators and real robots, with no privileged simulator inputs — that lets an out-of-the-box Qwen3.8-Flash-Next score 31.39 on RoboDojo, above the published GPT-6 Astra result of 28.97, and 53.50 across 19 real-world tasks, ahead of fine-tuned pi0.5. The analysis traces robot-use ability to four general capabilities: visual understanding, spatial grounding, geometric coding, and embodied reasoning.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01560) · [Project page](https://openrua.ai/uni-robot-agent)

**GroundingPI: A Grounding Foundation Model towards Physical Intelligence with Visual Primitives**

VLAs and world-action models inherit perception from general-purpose backbones that fail on precise, cluttered targets. GroundingPI is a 4B model that outputs points and boxes as quantized coordinates, trained with multimodal and spatial pretraining plus GRPO, and it averages 73.68% across 34 grounding benchmarks spanning 11 capabilities, above GPT-6 Astra's 71.54%. As a visual backbone it also improves manipulation data efficiency: on RoboCasa-GR1 it beats baselines trained on 75% of demonstrations while using only 50%. XPENG and academic partners announced the open release on November 4.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39601) · [Project page](https://groundingpi.github.io/) · [Code](https://github.com/groundingpi/GroundingPI)

## Hot Discussions

**Liber-0, a world-action model trained on human experience**

A November 4 post from LiberAI introduces Liber-0, a world-action model pretrained on more than 2.2 million hours of human experience, combining human video with glove-recorded hand motion and embodiment-conditioned learning to jointly predict future frames and actions. Across 60 GPT-6-proposed tasks it reports 36.0% overall zero-shot success — 63.5% on pick-and-place, 34.0% on other manipulation, and 10.5% on challenging long-horizon tasks — with the caveat that pretraining still included robot data, just not demonstrations of the evaluated tasks.

[View post](https://www.therobotreport.com/liberai-unveils-liber-0-a-world-action-model-that-grounds-human-experience-in-robot-action/)

**AIRoA-0 learns robot hands from handheld demonstrations alone**

A November 4 breakdown of AIRoA-0 describes a bimanual system trained on 16,151 hours of human demonstrations recorded with the handheld YUBI gripper, with no robot data at all during training — the robot appears only at test time, using only wrist cameras. The authors report 78% average success across 10 tasks, versus 27% for the strongest post-trained VLA baseline, and say the dataset, weights, and adaptation recipe will be released.

[View post](https://oossa.com/en/airoa-0-trains-robot-hands-from-handheld-demonstrations)

**VLAs do not know when not to act**

A November 3 explainer covers a recent VLA safety finding: given empty, nonsense, or impossible instructions in simulation, all six tested VLA models moved anyway, and five often completed a full task nobody assigned — and even an explicit "if no task is given, do not move" instruction stopped none of them. The takeaway is not rebellion but a missing do-nothing state, with a separate gate check suppressing most unintended starts but not fully solving the issue.

[View post](https://www.facebook.com/reel/2373533933392080/)

**ROSCon 2026 recap: agentic AI moves into ROS**

A November 4 recap of ROSCon 2026 highlights code-as-policy and repositories-as-policy, Meta's SAM 3 for text-prompted segmentation, VLAs moving from cloud to edge on compact hardware, and new ROS tooling including GPU memory transport between nodes, near-real-time reinforcement learning execution, and the Open Source Robotics Alliance's new Physical AI special interest group.

[View post](https://www.facebook.com/reel/29609192038698731/)
