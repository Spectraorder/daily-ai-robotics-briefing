# Daily AI Robotics Briefing — November 26, 2026

Thursday's edition leads with five fresh preprints from the November 26 arXiv listing — a steering prior that lifts a frozen π0.5 from 24.5% to 95.1% success, a cross-embodiment VLA family trained on over 10,000 hours, a probe showing world models cannot sense force, a safety-aware world-action model that imagines before it acts, and video priors transferred to cross-embodiment control — plus SenseTime's open robot-model release and two new podcast episodes.

## Papers

**Cairn: Object-Centric 3D Trajectory Priors for Steering Frozen VLAs**

VLAs tie where objects go to the layouts they were trained on, so moved objects and obstacles break them. Cairn keeps the VLA frozen and steers it at test time with a small prior that samples 3D trajectories for the object in the gripper, pulling the policy's denoising toward the next waypoint. On 1,941 held-out LIBERO scenes with displaced objects and obstacles, it lifts a frozen π0.5 from 24.5% to 95.1% success — above a π0.5 fine-tuned on the same layouts — and a simulation-trained prior guides a real Franka around an obstacle to a placement at the arm's reach limit.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34914)

**HoloBrain-0.5 Technical Report**

A family of cross-embodiment VLAs that plan in a shared action space, standardizing joint axes, coordinate frames, and tool-center points across robot descriptions and encoding kinematic graphs in a shared 3D representation. Pretrained on over 10,000 hours of real-robot, simulation, and human-manipulation data across 15 embodiment categories, the discrete variant reaches 56.77% mean success in simulation; after post-training, the continuous policy beats π0.5 on real-robot benchmarks (94.52% vs. 86.25%) and transfers zero-shot across Piper variants and, with limited demos, to the Unitree G1 humanoid.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34449)

![HoloBrain-0.5 overview: shared multimodal features and standardized robot state condition either the continuous diffusion policy or the discrete delayed-autoregressive policy](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34449v1/overview_redesigned.png)

*HoloBrain-0.5 overview (Figure 1 from the paper).*

**Can Visual World Models See Force?**

Manipulation depends on interaction force, a variable invisible in pixels — yet humans have a visual prior for it. Probing frozen image, video, world-model, and VLA representations with a lightweight readout trained on synchronized video and force measurements, the authors find most models can tell when contact happens but not how much force is being applied — and robot post-training does not fix it, exposing a blind spot in current world models and policies.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34422)

**WAtcher: Watching Ahead for Safe Robotic Manipulation with Efficient World-Action Models**

World-action models imagine the future but execute every action chunk blind to risk. WAtcher pairs a compact WAM — its video expert imagines outcomes in just two denoising steps — with Safe-Guard, a detachable 1.7M-parameter head that scores each candidate chunk from its imagined outcome and triggers a bounded retreat when risk is high. Trained on about 25,000 physics-labeled hazard windows, it cuts hazardous closed-loop trials from 27.0% to 10.8% without lowering success, and reaches 74.0% on five real-world tasks against 59.5% for π0.5.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34124) · [Project page](https://zyshan0929.github.io/WAtcher)

![WAtcher overview: a compact video expert imagines the outcome of a candidate chunk, and Safe-Guard predicts step- and chunk-level risk before the robot acts or retreats](https://zyshan0929.github.io/WAtcher/assets/images/fig-method.webp)

*WAtcher method overview (from the project page).*

**X-World: Transferring Video Priors to Cross-Embodiment Robot Control**

Action-labeled robot data is scarce; human and robot videos are plentiful. X-World adapts a pretrained video-diffusion backbone to variable camera views, compresses its features with a Q-Former into tokens for a shared flow-matching action expert, and post-trains on the target platform — running at 20 Hz with asynchronous chunking. It performs strongly on LIBERO and under zero-shot shifts on LIBERO-Plus, and averages 81.7% success across six bimanual tasks on a real dual-arm UR5e.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33914)

![X-World overview: the video backbone is adapted on robot and human videos, then a Q-Former compresses its features into tokens for the flow-matching action expert](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.33914v1/pipeline.png)

## Hot Discussions

**SenseTime's open robot model learns to choose between VLA and code**

SenseTime's full release of its open 27B robot vision-language model, announced November 25, lets the model decide on its own when to drive the arm with a VLA versus writing control code, and to self-correct from execution feedback. It scores 34.7 on RoboDojo's Agent protocol — up from 10.0 for the preview and above GPT-6 Astra's 27.6 — and the same weights stacked blocks 9 out of 10 times on a real Piper X arm with no real-robot training data.

[View post](https://www.threads.com/@bitbiztop/post/DgMs1FsG_jy) · [SenseTime announcement](https://www.sensetime.com/en/news/sensenova-roborsi-27b-full-release-20261126-1138)

**Agility's Digit solves a riddle with one language-model prompt**

Agility's Digit is making the rounds for solving a riddle-style instruction from a single language-model prompt: take the box matching the color of Darth Vader's lightsaber — red — and move it to the tallest tower in the front row, which it identifies as tower 4, then walks over and places it. A small demo, but a clean illustration of LLM reasoning driving a humanoid end to end.

[View post](https://www.threads.com/@eyeofthatguy/post/DgKkyO4D4md)

**Today's agentic-robotics harness study is already circulating**

A large-scale study of harness design for robot-use agents is being shared today: across 470,000 rollouts on 8 benchmarks, the way a VLM's reasoning is harnessed to VLA and world-action-model control shifts success by tens of points for the same models — the harness, not just the model, is the variable that matters.

[View post](https://www.threads.com/@unframer.hoya/post/DgMrkNsGpDD) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34778)
