# Daily AI Robotics Briefing — October 30, 2026

Friday's edition is about making robot learning cheaper and more general: the largest open subtask-annotated bimanual dataset yet, a humanoid VLA family trained largely on egocentric human experience, and a sub-100M-parameter latent world-action model that beats billion-parameter rivals — plus a multisensory humanoid demo and a two-brain VLA setup circulating on social feeds.

## Papers

**FineART: Fine-Grained Annotated Robotic Trajectory Dataset and Vision-Language-Action Model for Bimanual Manipulation**

Scale AI and Hugging Face's LeRobot team open-sourced FineART on October 29: 40,543 bimanual episodes (1,718 hours, 151 tasks) carrying 533,913 human subtask labels, about 13 per episode. Its FineART-VLA policy predicts its own next subtask before acting, lifting spatial-instruction success from 32.0% to 100.0% after subtask mid-training and long-horizon success with human subtask guidance from 16.0% to 76.0%, while matching baseline performance on a new robot with 10x less fine-tuning data.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36416) · [Announcement](https://labs.scale.com/blog/fineart) · [Dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/lerobot/FineART)

**HuGe: Scaling Humanoid Generalist Vision-Language-Action Models with Egocentric Experience**

Humanoid demonstrations are expensive, so HuGe learns whole-body control largely from egocentric human experience: over 2,000 hours of human and robot data feed three VLA architectures that predict latent motion tokens for a pretrained whole-body controller, via a three-stage pipeline of egocentric pre-training, humanoid adaptation, and downstream fine-tuning. Simulation playback supplies missing robot proprioception during pre-training and arm re-rendering bridges the visual gap later; the best variant reaches 66.67% on HumanoidArena, and adapted human demos lift a real Unitree G1 pick-and-drop task.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34186) · [Project page](https://yliu-cs.github.io/HuGe) · [Code](https://github.com/yliu-cs/HuGe)

**GAWM: A Compact Latent World-Action Model for Multi-Task Robot Manipulation**

Most world-action models lean on billion-parameter backbones; GAWM does the job with under 100M parameters and no robot or video pretraining. A frozen DINOv3 encoder compresses each camera view into 64 tokens, a small task-conditioned predictor forecasts those tokens at two future horizons, and an action decoder regresses the action chunk from current and forecast tokens. It scores 95.6% on LIBERO and 93.9% / 93.1% on RoboTwin 2.0 (clean / randomized), beating billion-parameter world-action models there, and predicts dual-arm actions more accurately than a fine-tuned billion-parameter VLA at far lower latency.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37091v1) · [Project page](https://oucliuyang.github.io/gawm-homepage/) · [Code](https://github.com/gaoxiang12/ga-wm)

## Hot Discussions

**NEURA's Neuron AI demo: a multisensory "perception-language-action" brain**

A demo reel for NEURA Robotics' Neuron AI shows dexterous hand work — unscrewing a cap, picking small parts, clicking components together, reaching into a bag without looking — while its presenters argue that touch, sound, and feel, not vision alone, tell the robot when parts connect or what an object is by feel.

[View post](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfIE_bSEQfP/)

**A two-brain VLA plays tic-tac-toe**

A home-workshop demo pairs a large model as the "boss" brain, which reads the board from an overhead camera and picks a move, with SmolVLA as the "worker" brain that turns the command into arm motion — a concrete, small-scale illustration of the slow-planner / fast-policy split running through this week's VLA work.

[View post](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfCWMfqiL39/)

**XPENG's IRON humanoid follows gestures in real time**

A promo for XPENG's IRON humanoid shows it watching a person walk up and extend a hand, then responding with a handshake, with the maker crediting "VLA 2.0" for continuously turning observed gestures into motion without spoken commands.

[View post](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfB2hvbxxQY/)
