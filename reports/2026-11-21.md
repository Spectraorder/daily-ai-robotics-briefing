## Papers

**Awaken-HI: Scaling Humanoid Intelligence with Whole-Body Human Experience**

Humanoid pretraining has been starved of whole-body motion data, so EngineAI built Awaken-Human: roughly 50,000 hours of egocentric stereo video paired with synchronized whole-body human motion across more than 100 skills. Their VLA, Awaken-HI, jointly predicts the original human motion and the retargeted robot actions through a shared action expert, and more human pretraining data follows an empirical scaling law — after full-corpus pretraining it averages 93.75% success across eight real-robot tasks, beating GR00T N1.7 and π0.5.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26420) · [Project page](https://engineai-robotics.github.io/Awaken-HI/)

**PhyDEX: A Benchmark for Physical Reasoning in Vision-Language-Action Models via Dynamic Extrinsic Dexterity**

Most manipulation benchmarks test gripper–object contact and ignore physics, so PhyDEX tests it directly: a robot must throw an object to a destination beyond its reach, which requires inferring the object's mass, center of mass, and friction. Across 35K trajectories, nine tasks, and 100 scenes, GR00T N1.7, DynamicVLA, and ForceVLA all score below 3% — and giving a policy the ground-truth physical properties roughly doubles its success, showing the bottleneck is physical understanding, not just control.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26565)

**Risk-Controlled Test-Time Adaptation for Vision-Language-Action Models**

Retrieval-augmented VLAs adapt at test time by conditioning on expert demonstrations, but even relevant demos don't guarantee the policy uses them well. R2-VLA adds a learned uncertainty predictor that decides, at each step, whether to act autonomously or ask an expert — and calibrates that threshold so a user-specified task success rate is met with minimal expert help, folding corrections back into the retrieval memory as it goes. Across three VLA backbones and four LIBERO suites it lifts average success by 9.2–11.2 percentage points over the retrieval-only baseline.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26438)

**AgentRoboCritic: Understanding the Capability of Frontier Agents on Direct Robot Control**

Frontier agents like GPT-6 Astra can now drive robots with low-level numerical actions and no task-specific training, but episode success rates hide how they succeed and where they fail. AgentRoboCritic audits them action by action: the policy states a local intent alongside each action, and a critic judges the outcome and failure mode against that intent across 12 manipulation benchmarks. The agents recover from local failures in closed loop and ground reliably, but grasping and alignment remain the physical bottlenecks — and the released dataset of 12,844 annotated actions plus a fine-tuned open critic (failure-mode accuracy up from 32.58% to 74.24%) makes the analysis cheap to repeat.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26613) · [Project page](https://adu2021.github.io/blog/AgentRoboCritic/)

![Intent-grounded action-level critique: the policy agent states a local intent with each action, and the critic judges the outcome and failure mode against it](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.26613v1/fig2_pipeline.png)

*The AgentRoboCritic pipeline (Figure 2 from the paper).*

**World Models Favor Learned Priors over Visual Evidence**

World models trained on long-tailed data default to the most common dynamics, even when a short calibration video shows a rarer rule is in play. TailWorld isolates that bias by controlling training imbalance and calibration informativeness independently, and RIFT — which infers the governing rule from calibration interactions while explicitly controlling the influence of training frequency — improves prediction accuracy and physical consistency across distribution shifts, including on rare dynamics.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26366)

## Hot Discussions

**NVIDIA's robotics chief: VLAs alone aren't enough — enter "world action models"**

A November 21 Threads clip from The Information's AI Deep Dive has Deepu Talla, NVIDIA's VP of Robotics and Edge AI, arguing that because a robot's action changes the world, it needs a world foundation model like Cosmos to predict outcomes before acting — the field is moving toward "world action models" that fuse prediction and action into one system.

[View post](https://www.threads.com/@theinformation/post/DgAZSlOu6iC)

**One brain, many bodies: Gemini 2.5 vs GR00T N1.7 vs Helix 2.5**

A November 21 explainer compares the three leading "one brain, many bodies" VLA approaches — DeepMind's partners-only Gemini Robotics 2.5, NVIDIA's open GR00T N1.7, and Figure's in-house Helix 2.5 — and closes with the caveat that all the headline numbers are vendor-reported, with no shared benchmark across them.

[View post](https://www.threads.com/@thetechjourneys/post/DgA1qx0zuto)

**Z-sight: foresight without the video-rendering bill**

A November 21 reel breaks down Z-sight, which unifies VLA control with latent world modeling by predicting future visual tokens directly in the VLM's own token space instead of generating pixel video — 73.3% average success across six real Unitree G1 tasks at about 110 ms per chunk, up from 53.3% for Fast-WAM.

[View post](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DgAEq8FFBUD/) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24940)

**Agentic robots that write their own code**

A November 21 IEEE Spectrum post covers UC Berkeley and NVIDIA researchers building "agentic robots" in which large language models write human-readable code to control robots, rather than training on massive datasets.

[View post](https://www.threads.com/@ieeespectrum/post/DgAjpUaG3s-)
