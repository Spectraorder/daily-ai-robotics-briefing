# Daily AI Robotics Briefing — October 23, 2026

A world-model day with a humanoid twist: 3D scene flow joins vision and action as a co-equal modality, self-distillation sharpens single-step future representations, and skeleton supervision grounds latent actions in robot motion — plus a memory that keeps the cues a walking humanoid has already turned away from, and view routing that follows the task stage.

## Papers

**Skeleton-Grounded Latent Action Model for Robot World Models**

SkeLAM splits latent actions into a kinematic component grounded by robot-skeleton supervision and a residual component for the rest of the visual dynamics, so the world model learns a clean target for mapping real robot actions into latent space. On DROID it cuts end-effector error by 19.5% and LPIPS by 11.1% over DreamDojo, and shows zero-shot human-to-robot motion transfer in generated rollouts.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31474) · [Project page](https://aopolin-lv.github.io/SkeLAM/)

**UniFlowWAM: A Unified World-Action Model with Visual Semantics and 4D Geometry**

UniFlowWAM makes scene-level 3D flow — how every point of the scene moves in 3D — a third modality on par with vision and action, with one diffusion-transformer expert per modality exchanging information only through joint attention. A ten-objective role grid lets the same model act as policy, forward/inverse dynamics, or predictor, lifting RoboCasa-GR1 from 52.1% to 56.4%, holding LIBERO at 98.1% (87.7% zero-shot on LIBERO-Plus), and reaching 87.0% on real bimanual tasks versus 68.5% for the strongest baseline.

![UniFlowWAM method overview: current and future observations, point map and scene flow, and proprioception with action chunks are encoded and fed to a three-expert diffusion transformer with joint self-attention](https://kksham111.github.io/UniFlowWAM_ProjectPage/assets/img/pipeline.jpg)

*UniFlowWAM's method: vision, 3D flow, and action experts meet only in joint self-attention, and deployment needs no flow input.*

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30991) · [Project page](https://kksham111.github.io/UniFlowWAM_ProjectPage/)

**Self-Distilling Future Video Representations for World Action Models**

FRED targets the bottleneck in single-step world action models: action quality depends on future video representations generated from noise in one denoising pass. A momentum teacher distills representations from noisy ground-truth futures into the student, with a short observation history as temporal context — lifting long-horizon success from 61.7% to 81.7%, out-of-domain success from 10.4% to 52.1% at the 14B scale, and human-to-robot transfer from 19.4% to 50.0%.

![FRED method overview: student generates a future representation from noise while a momentum teacher produces the target from a noisy ground-truth future observation, both conditioned on observation history](https://huiwon-jang.github.io/FRED/static/images/fig1_overview.png)

*FRED's method: self-distillation plus history conditioning sharpens the single-step future representation the action decoder reads.*

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30320) · [Project page](https://huiwon-jang.github.io/FRED/) · [Code](https://github.com/suhyeok-jang/FRED/)

**Memory before Goal: Role of Pre-History Memory for Humanoid Loco-Manipulation**

WholeBodyMem gives whole-body VLA policies the memory they lack in dynamic scenes: SAM3 tracks task objects, and object events — appearing, disappearing, moving, changing — causally select the key frames an action expert reads by cross-attention, rather than uniform sampling. It raises its base policy from 29.1% to 72.6% in simulation and from 36.7% to 88.3% on a real Unitree G1, alongside a new HuMeGo benchmark of six simulated and six real memory-dependent tasks.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31166) · [Project page](https://memory-before-goal.github.io)

**FocusVLA: Future-Intent Guided Dynamic View Routing for Vision-Language-Action Models**

FocusVLA diagnoses "view-dependency inertia": multi-view VLAs keep leaning on the wrist view even when a task stage calls for the global view. It predicts future interaction intent from observations and action history and uses it to route global and local views step by step during action generation, without modifying the VLA backbone — gaining 10.10 points over π₀.₅ on RoboTwin 2.0 and 13.3 points (71.7% to 85.0%) in the real world.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31423)
