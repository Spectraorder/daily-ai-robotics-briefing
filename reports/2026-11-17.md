# Daily AI Robotics Briefing — November 17, 2026

Tuesday's edition opens with the largest VLA ever built at 552 billion parameters, a world-action model that rethinks before it acts, force feedback for frozen frontier models, humanoid motion training in under a minute, and a benchmark showing video world models fail to track physical state — plus AMD absorbing World Labs, NVIDIA's FLORA data engine, a tactile foundation model, and a Latent Space episode on visual intelligence.

## Papers

**ScaleVLA: Scaling Vision-Language-Action Models to 552 Billion Parameters**

The largest VLA to date connects a 552B DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash VLM backbone to a trainable flow-matching action DiT. With just 10 demonstrations it beats a 2B backbone trained on 50 (87.8% vs 85.6% on LIBERO-Goal), reaches 45.1% on LIBERO-PRO against at most 31.3% for smaller backbones, and lifts real-world charger insertion from 4/10 to 9/10, at 216 ms median latency per action chunk.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21607) · [Project page](https://galaxygeneralrobotics.github.io/scalevla/)

![The ScaleVLA architecture: a VLM encodes images and instruction, and an action DiT generates the action chunk](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.21607v1/architecture.png)

**ThinkWAM: Rethinking Latent Futures for Action Refinement**

A latent world-action model that rethinks before acting: it proposes a full action chunk, corrects multi-horizon future latents with a learned residual conditioned on that proposal, then re-runs the same action head for the executable chunk. It hits 98.70% on LIBERO, 84.76% on LIBERO-Plus without out-of-distribution fine-tuning, and 64.4% mean success across three real-world out-of-distribution conditions on a Galaxea A1Z.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21258) · [Project page](https://thinkwam-project.github.io/) · [Code](https://github.com/HaoyunT/Thinkwam)

![The ThinkWAM architecture: temporal JEPA, affordance decoder, and a two-pass Action DiT](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.21258v1/architecture.png)

*The ThinkWAM architecture: temporal JEPA, affordance decoder, and a two-pass Action DiT (Figure 2 from the paper).*

**ForceHarness: Turning Force into Action and Physics for Frozen Frontier Models**

Frozen frontier models can now use contact force with no demonstrations or fine-tuning: self-terminating force primitives close the force loop beneath the model, and a physics translation layer returns each force trace as physical quantities with uncertainty. Zero-shot success for GPT-6 Astra on a real Franka arm (no wrist force sensor) jumps from 3.3% to 90.0%, and it matches hand-written expert controllers on the new HiddenPhys benchmark.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21099) · [Project page](https://showlab.github.io/ForceHarness) · [Code](https://github.com/showlab/ForceHarness)

![The ForceHarness overview: a frozen model calls force primitives, and a physics translation layer returns physical quantities](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.21099v1/fig2_overview.png)

*The ForceHarness overview: a frozen model calls force primitives, and a physics translation layer returns physical quantities (Figure 2 from the paper).*

**XMimic and XSoma: Learning Humanoid Motion Tracking and Locomotion in Under a Minute**

A JAX rebuild of SuperMimic-style world-model humanoid training that compiles whole iterations into XLA programs and splits them across up to 4 H100s. XMimic tracks all 40 LAFAN1 clips on a Unitree G1 in 0.86 minutes (3.7× BlinkMimic); XSoma reaches Holosoma's rough-terrain locomotion target in 0.77 minutes (12.3× FastSAC, using one-eighth the environment steps). It directly follows yesterday's CoRL Best Paper.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20452) · [Code (XMimic)](https://github.com/nico-bohlinger/xmimic) · [Code (XSoma)](https://github.com/nico-bohlinger/xsoma)

**The Flip Test: Video World Models Fail to Learn the World State**

A counterfactual benchmark (1,680 initial frames, 25 simulated and real tasks) testing whether video world models track physical state: a tiny change that flips the true outcome should flip the prediction. All 11 evaluated video world models fail — the best averages 18.3% pair accuracy, below the 25% random-guessing baseline — while a VLM prompted on the same frames is selective. A direct challenge to video prediction as world modeling.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19822) · [Project page](https://romyluo.com/blog/fliptest)

## Hot Discussions

**AMD closes the World Labs acquisition; Fei-Fei Li becomes chief scientist**

AMD announced November 16 it has completed its acquisition of World Labs, bringing the world-model lab's 3D-environment and robot-learning/simulation technology inside AMD. Fei-Fei Li joins as executive vice president and chief scientist, reporting to CEO Lisa Su, while the World Labs team continues its foundational model research alongside AMD engineers.

[Article](https://newsroom.amd.com/news/amd-completes-acquisition-world-labs/)

**NVIDIA's FLORA data engine gets a Threads walkthrough**

A November 16 Threads video demos FLORA, NVIDIA's open model suite that auto-labels robot and human manipulation video into training data — splitting actions, writing instructions, and judging success. NVIDIA claims 200,000 hours labeled per day at low cost. FLORA itself was released October 20, so this is new discussion of it, not a new release.

[View post](https://www.threads.com/@simplifyinai/post/Dfzo0BniQb6)

**Ant Group's LingBot-Touch tactile model discussed on Facebook**

A November 16 reel explains LingBot-Touch, Robbyant's open tactile foundation model trained on 4,983 hours of touch data across three sensor types, which lifted four robot policies from 48.3% to 73.1% mean success across 26 real-world tasks. The underlying preprint is from November 9; the November 16 item is the discussion.

[View post](https://www.facebook.com/reel/1694336512203004/)
