# Daily AI Robotics Briefing — November 3, 2026

Tuesday's edition is a full-stack day: a co-designed humanoid system and its human-demonstration pipeline, a test-time selector that checks whether a world-action model's imagined future matches its own action, a navigation world model pretrained on action-free video, and a distributed evaluation network that puts VLAs and frontier agents on real robots in the wild — plus a fleet of Figure humanoids walking out of the factory, a robot brain that learns on the job, and physical AI at FedEx scale.

## Papers

**Pixel: A Co-Designed Full-Stack Robot System for Learning General-Purpose Mobile Manipulation**

Most robot systems bolt hardware, data collection, control, and learning together after the fact. Pixel co-designs them: a low-cost wheeled humanoid with human-aligned kinematics and compliant grippers, teleoperation plus robot-free human data interfaces, a unified whole-body controller, and a shared policy pipeline across human and robot demonstrations — with a hierarchical stack where a VLM plans subtasks and a flow-matching policy executes them, demonstrated on dexterous, active-perception, navigation, and whole-body tasks.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03648) · [Project page](https://pixel-robot.com/pixel)

![Pixel policy architecture showing a high-level VLM planning subtasks that condition a DiT action expert](https://pixel-robot.com/figures/policy-architecture.svg)

*Figure: Pixel's method — a high-level VLM plans subtasks from voice, context, and keyframes, and a DiT action expert turns them into actions under a shared whole-body controller.*

**Pixel-UMI: Learning Environment-Aware Whole-Body Manipulation from Sensorized Human Demonstrations**

Human demonstrations scale, but the human body is not the robot body. Pixel-UMI retargets sensorized human demos with reconstructed scene geometry so the robot's posture avoids obstacles, masks camera views that retargeting corrupted, and refines the policy with on-robot human corrections via DAgger — reaching 87.0% average task progress across five articulated, deformable, constrained, and contact-rich bimanual tasks.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03647) · [Project page](https://pixel-robot.com/pixel-umi)

**Seeing Through the Illusion: Test-Time Scaling for World Action Models**

A world-action model can imagine a beautiful future its action could never produce. WISE, a frozen-model selector, scores each sampled rollout on action validity, inverse-dynamics consistency between the proposed action and the imagined motion, and instruction-conditioned task progress, then executes the best candidate — lifting closed-loop success from 6.0% to 24.0% on RoboLab with Cosmos 3 Edge and from 75.1% to 78.3% on LIBERO with DreamZero.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03585)

**OpenNWM: A Generalist Navigation World Model with Latent Action Pretraining**

Navigation world models are starved of action-labeled data. OpenNWM first learns universal latent actions from action-free video — curating NavAnywhere, 17.5 million observations from 15 sources spanning ground robots, humans, and drones — then aligns those latents with real motions, improving both visual prediction and navigation in and out of distribution.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01844) · [Project page](https://openspace-lab.github.io/OpenNWM/) · [Code](https://github.com/OpenSpace-Lab/OpenNWM)

![OpenNWM method overview showing a latent action encoder and decoder plus a conditional diffusion transformer predicting future frames](https://openspace-lab.github.io/OpenNWM/assets/paper/method-overview-dark-20261031.svg)

*Figure: OpenNWM's method — latent actions inferred from observations condition a diffusion transformer that predicts future frames for navigation.*

**RoboColosseum: Evaluating Physical Intelligence at Scale In-The-Wild**

Simulation leaderboards saturate, so RoboColosseum runs a distributed network of locally operated robots under one protocol: 5,699 blinded pairwise comparisons across 124 sites, testing five VLAs and three frontier agents on four embodiments. Frontier agents already rival specialized VLAs, no policy completes any G1 humanoid task, and fine-tuning reshuffles the rankings — GR00T N1.7 leads on SO-101, pi0.5 on Franka and bimanual YAM — a warning that open-track rankings poorly predict adaptability.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02497) · [Project page](https://robocolosseum.ai/)

## Hot Discussions

**Figure 03 robots walk out of BotQ**

A November 2 post from Figure's CEO shows newly manufactured Figure 03 humanoids leaving the BotQ factory and walking across campus, one crossing a pedestrian crossing while others follow — a manufacturing-fleet glimpse rather than a new capability claim, with no production total or control details attached.

[View post](https://www.humanoidsdaily.com/news/figure-03-robots-leave-botq-campus-video)

**Agibot's GO-3, the robot brain that learns on the job**

A November 3 breakdown of Agibot's October 29 GO-3 preview describes a three-stage loop — broad pretraining on human videos and robot trajectories, bulk practice via Genie Evolver across about 100 robots and simulators, then on-the-job learning as Genie Master logs failures — with a claimed success-rate climb from 28.6% to 85.7% in five days, flagged as Agibot's own unvalidated benchmarks with open source promised by end of 2026.

[View post](https://www.instagram.com/p/DfSj7k0D8GJ/)

**ABB's robot stack becomes an orchestrator of AI agents**

A November 3 stage demo presents ABB's stack as three cooperating agents — perception that segments the scene, a language model that parses requests, and a planner that writes the program — with skills trained by VLA-style imitation of human hand movements, shown pouring water over coffee grounds, and pitched as deployed industrial technology rather than a lab demo.

[View post](https://www.facebook.com/reel/984727447467063/)

**Viam post-trains Cosmos for a robot arm**

A November 3 post from Viam describes fine-tuning NVIDIA's Cosmos 3 Edge on Viam data so a ULite 6 arm picks up a red block in simulation, with a live walkthrough promised for November 5 — a small, concrete example of world-model post-training aimed at one robot skill rather than a general demo.

[View post](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfSRaMrx0_Q/)
