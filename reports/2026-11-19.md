# Daily AI Robotics Briefing — November 19, 2026

Thursday's edition leads with a unified VLA–world-model foundation model, a scene-structure world-action model, a video world model that renders the robot to predict the world, a fix for bumped cameras that breaks frozen VLAs, and diffusion policies learned from human play — plus OpenVLA 2, JEPA Dots, and XPENG's second-gen VLA teaser.

## Papers

**Z-sight: Unifying Vision-Language-Action and Latent World Modeling**

A unified robot foundation model that couples a VLA with generative latent world modeling: instead of reconstructing pixels, Z-sight predicts the future directly in the VLM's visual token space, with foresight and action experts jointly denoising future spatial tokens and actions. It reaches 81.9% success on SIMPLE (+9.6 points over the strongest baseline) and 73.3% across six Unitree G1 loco-manipulation tasks (+20.0 points) at 110 ms per chunk.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24940)

**Phoenix: Learning Scene Structure Representations for Generalizable World-Action Models**

World-action models usually learn reconstruction-oriented representations that ignore scene structure — Phoenix fixes that by distilling geometric and spatiotemporal knowledge into a DINOv3-based encoder, compressing it with a causal VAE, then jointly predicting scene latents and actions. Pretrained on roughly 39,000 hours of egocentric and robot data, it hits 94.8% on LIBERO-Plus and 72.4% on RoboTwin 2.0 C2R, with strong few-shot adaptation and cross-embodiment transfer.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24938)

![The Phoenix architecture: dual-teacher distillation into the Phoenix Encoder, CS-VAE latent compression, and joint latent-action prediction](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.24938v1/architecture.png)

**Render the Robot, Generate the World: One Video World Model Across Embodiments**

Doppel conditions a video world model on camera-aligned renderings of the robot's future body configurations plus numerical action trajectories, so the model only has to predict object motion and interaction effects — and renderings give a common visual interface across embodiments. One shared backbone trained on eight datasets spanning eight embodiments (about 600 hours of video) improves PSNR over action-only baselines by 1.00 dB on held-out trajectories and 1.20 dB out of distribution.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24925) · [Project page](https://doppelwm.github.io/) · [Code](https://github.com/DoppelWM/Doppel)

![The Doppel architecture: rendered future body configurations and action trajectories condition a shared video backbone](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.24925v1/Arch.png)

**Homeward: No Place Like the Training View**

Frozen VLAs assume the third-person camera never moves — bump it and success collapses. Homeward is a training-free, plug-and-play adapter that canonicalizes observations from an uncalibrated deployment camera back into the training view, estimating the unknown camera and scene depth from a live frame while rendering the moving robot links directly from its kinematic model. Across roughly 300,000 closed-loop episodes and 1,599 camera variants on LIBERO-Plus, it lifts OpenVLA-OFT from 62.5% to 77.6% and π0.5 from 76.5% to 95.1%.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24455)

![Overview of Homeward: monocular scene restoration plus kinematic robot rendering returns observations to the training view](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.24455v1/homeward.png)

*Overview of Homeward: monocular scene restoration plus kinematic robot rendering returns observations to the training view (Figure 2 from the paper).*

**DiWALI: Diffusion Policy Learning in World Models via Latent Action Inference from Human and Robot Play**

Robot data is scarce, but humans can demo a skill in seconds — without robot actions. DiWALI infers latent actions from consecutive frames with an inverse dynamics model, trains a world model on action-free human and robot play, and grounds the latents in robot commands with a small decoder. On CALVIN, policies trained solely from human demonstrations beat baselines trained with robot demos, and RL in imagination improves them further on a physical robot.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24836) · [Project page](https://diwali.cs.uni-freiburg.de)

## Hot Discussions

**OpenVLA 2 is out**

The OpenVLA consortium released OpenVLA 2 on November 17: an open-weight VLA with a reported 30% gain in task generalization over 1.0, an adaptive reasoning module that adjusts actions from real-time visual feedback, multi-robot coordination under one language model, and low-latency edge inference — with the weights staying fully open-source.

[Article](https://civl.com/news/story/openvla-consortium-releases-openvla-2-for-humanoid-robotics-649f934e) · [View post](https://www.threads.com/@toris.kr/post/Df4w_BCDFeM)

**JEPA Dots circulates on Threads**

A November 19 Threads post shares JEPA Dots, a dual-latent world model from today's arXiv listing that learns visual dynamics separately from task representations to enable language-guided robot control.

[View post](https://www.threads.com/@sipirtu/post/Df6ojJdDvb3) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24062)
