# Daily AI Robotics Briefing — November 24, 2026

Tuesday's edition leads with five fresh preprints from the November 24 arXiv listing — world-action models that run in real time, a world model turned into a latent-action teacher, a native embodied world system, 3D relational grounding for VLAs, and whole-body humanoid motion priors — plus Embo's $110M seed round, XPENG's cross-embodiment WorldGPT, and the 552B-parameter ScaleVLA making the rounds on social.

## Papers

**VITA-WAM: Manifold-Aligned World-Action Modeling for Real-Time Robot Control**

World-action models are powerful but slow: they denoise from uninformative Gaussian noise over many ODE steps and re-inject task context through expensive cross-attention. VITA-WAM aligns the latent observation and action manifolds so flow matching transports observation representations straight into latent actions, needing just one ODE step — beating 10-step FastWAM by 21.47 points on LIBERO-Plus at 55.67 ms latency, a 2.10× speedup, with no distillation.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31195)

![Architecture of VITA-WAM: during training a Q-Former fuses visual, language, and proprioceptive inputs into a latent source, and flow latent alignment maps it to latent actions](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.31195v1/figures/Fig2_Training_Inference_Architecture.png)

*Architecture of VITA-WAM (Figure 2 from the paper).*

**World Models as Native Latent Action Models**

Latent action models recover dynamics from action-less video but train their inverse and forward dynamics from scratch; pretrained video world models already generate high-fidelity dynamics but keep them entangled across layers. Native LAM runs one pretrained video diffusion transformer as both inverse and forward dynamics, with learnable dynamics registers as the only causal bridge, distilling compact native latent actions from counterfactual temporal pairs. Its PAWlicy pretraining reaches 93.6% average success on RoboTwin 2.0 with a 3.8B model, and the latent actions replay across robots, scenes, and human-to-robot transfer.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30898)

![Training Native LAM: each video is paired with a retimed copy sharing the first frame but not the motion, and dynamics registers distill the motion into a compact native latent action](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.30898v1/method.png)

*Training Native LAM (Figure 2 from the paper).*

**Innata-0: A Native Embodied World System with Model-Agent Co-Design**

Standalone foundation models struggle with long-horizon execution, and bolting an agent onto a model creates inconsistencies between planning and control. Innata-0 co-designs the two: a tri-tower mixture-of-transformers world-action model integrating understanding, prediction, and action generation, plus an agent that coordinates long-horizon execution through memory, verification, and recovery — with a co-design protocol aligning model training with agent execution so validated experience feeds back into adaptation.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30254)

**WeaveVLA: 3D Relational Grounding for Vision-Language-Action Models**

VLAs understand 3D relations between objects and the gripper only implicitly, which hurts spatial reasoning during manipulation. WeaveVLA makes it explicit: a task-conditioned sparse graph neural network encodes relative 3D relations, pair-specific recurrent edge states carry geometric evidence across observations, and a learned-query readout turns the graph into relational tokens that condition the action expert. Built on StarVLA-GR00T and π0.5, it tops 98% on LIBERO, lifts LIBERO-Plus from 74.9% to 82.0% and 85.7% to 87.8%, and improves real-world success from 55.6% to 70.0% (33.3% to 53.3% under out-of-distribution perturbations).

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31126)

![Architecture of WeaveVLA: object-level visual features and relative 3D geometry form a task-conditioned relational graph, encoded by graph transformer blocks into relational tokens for the action expert](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.31126v1/graphvla_v2.png)

*Architecture of WeaveVLA (Figure 2 from the paper).*

**CHAMP: Coordinated Humanoid Loco-Manipulation with Whole-Body Motion Priors**

Coordinating walking and dexterous hands is a core humanoid challenge. CHAMP pretrains a vision-language model to predict future body and hand motion from about 2,298 hours of heterogeneous human and robot data, sharing one motion representation across embodiments, then grounds it in control with a Coordination-oriented Action Expert that sparsely routes and fuses features from multiple pretrained layers. It reaches 84.8% across seven simulated tasks and 91.4% across seven real-world tasks on a Unitree G1 — including 70.0% on long-horizon popcorn serving versus 40.0% for the best baseline.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30449)

![Model architecture of CHAMP: a VLM is pretrained to predict future body and hand motion tokens, then a coordination-oriented action expert grounds the motion priors in robot control](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.30449v1/model_architecture.png)

## Hot Discussions

**Embo raises a $110M seed to build a world-model robot brain**

Dreamer creator Danijar Hafner and Wilson Yan's new startup Embo announced a $110M seed round led by Andreessen Horowitz, with Khosla Ventures, DST Global, and NVIDIA's NVentures participating, to build one unified world model — pretrained on internet-scale video, fine-tuned on humanoid fleet experience, and post-trained with RL in imagined rollouts. No model, benchmarks, or launch date yet; the funding is the news, and the bet is that video-learned physics plus imagined practice beats task-by-task training.

[View post](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DgH-X79Rlqx/) · [Dealroom report](https://dealroom.co/news/175093-embo-raises-110m-seed-to-build-robot-brains-with-world-models/)

**XPENG unveils WorldGPT, one world-action model across six embodiments**

XPENG announced WorldGPT today, a unified cross-embodiment world-action model in its Turing World Model family that takes RGB video and text instructions and outputs future video, actions, vision tasks, and reasoning across cars, boats, drones, single-arm robots, dual-arm robots, and humanoids — learning shared physics in one cloud teacher model, then distilling it into on-device students. The company claims 12 first-place public benchmark rankings; no public access or open weights were announced.

[View post](https://www.threads.com/@lucasthcho/post/DgIZRUaDbDV) · [XPENG announcement](https://www.xpeng.com/news/01a20bcf44b3a1e838d68a02a2110153)

**ScaleVLA: a 552B-parameter brain for robot policies**

A November 23 reel breaks down ScaleVLA (arXiv:2611.21607, November 16), which plugs a frozen 552B DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash VLM into a trainable action diffusion transformer and compares backbones from 2B to 552B: 10 demonstrations beat a 2B model trained on 50 (87.8% vs. 85.6% on LIBERO-Goal), LIBERO-PRO generalization reaches 45.1% versus at most 31.3% for smaller brains, and real-world charger insertion improves from 4/10 to 9/10 — at 216 ms per action chunk on four H200 GPUs.

[View post](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DgFcA3wBF3A/) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21607)

**SANA-Robot: a world-action model with minute-level memory that runs on the edge**

A November 24 post walks through SANA-Robot (arXiv:2611.28943, November 23), NVIDIA and collaborators' 4.5B world-action model that keeps minute-long episode memory at constant cost via hybrid memory — Gated DeltaNet layers for compressed global state plus softmax layers for exact recent context — hitting 75% on the three-cup shell game (vs. 30% without memory) and 115 ms per action chunk on an RTX 5090 for 30 Hz closed-loop control.

[View post](https://www.facebook.com/groups/2647662409012090/permalink/2647666315678366/) · [Project page](https://nvlabs.github.io/Sana/Robot/)
