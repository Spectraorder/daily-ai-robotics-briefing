# Daily AI Robotics Briefing — November 12, 2026

Thursday's edition is a world-model double-header: a unified meta-frame that folds every robot signal into a video diffusion model's prior, a three-episode fix for world models whose physics drifted, and a shared intent codec that moves skills across robot bodies — plus CoRL's final day in Austin, where Fei-Fei Li's Atlas keynote is still echoing and the workshops take over.

## Papers

**From Video Frames to Robot Meta-Frames: A Unified Latent Interface for World Action Models**

World action models need to inject low-dimensional robot signals — proprioception, actions, values — into a latent space shaped by natural video, and existing schemes spend a separate latent frame per signal or bolt on dedicated encoders. MetaWAM folds every signal into a single unified meta-frame, assigning each to a disjoint frequency-domain band lifted by an inverse DCT, so the interface stays aligned with the pretrained video diffusion prior with no added modules. It reaches 68.9% success on RoboCasa while cutting inference latency 39%, and 85.0% on three real-world Franka tasks.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14365) · [Code](https://github.com/showlab/MetaWAM)

**Few-Shot Residual Adaptation of Pretrained Latent World Models**

Frozen latent world models break when deployment physics shift — a changed center of mass or friction can drive success near zero. This work learns self-supervised residual corrections from latent prediction errors, applied both inside the latent predictor and on its output, while the pretrained model stays frozen. From just three interaction episodes, the dual-site approach recovers 90% success under a center-of-mass shift and 100% under modified friction on a real robot. A practical answer to the question of how a deployed world model adapts without retraining.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13592)

**CIALLO: Learning Shared Intent Representations for Cross-Embodiment Manipulation**

Robot demonstrations hold reusable manipulation knowledge, but transferring it across bodies means bridging different control interfaces. CIALLO is an action codec that aligns actions, visual changes, and observation-grounded language in a shared space of short-horizon behavioral intent, with an embodiment-conditioned decoder mapping intent back to robot-specific actions. Pretrained on eight embodiments, it adapts to a new robot from a single recording of at most 10 seconds. Qwen-CIALLO reaches 76.7% average success on real SO-101 and Piper robots with 20 demonstrations per task, beating a fully fine-tuned π0 (70.0%), and 97.3% on LIBERO.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13732)

**From Egocentric Human Video to Robot Manipulation in the Era of Embodied Intelligence**

Egocentric human video is a potentially scalable source of manipulation experience — if you can recover supervision without recorded robot actions. This survey organizes Ego2Robot learning into four paradigms: explicit embodiment transfer, explicit action and task supervision, egocentric video representation pretraining, and latent action and dynamics modeling, tracing what each extracts from human video and when the extra data actually helps. It also maps the benchmarks and the open challenges: physical feasibility, agent coordination, in-context adaptation, and controlled evaluation. A useful map for anyone betting on human video as robot data.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13610)

**PhysMoE: Fast Spatial Grounding with Single-Token Coordinate Representation in Modality-Decoupled VLMs**

Embodied VLMs are the backbone of robot planning, but coordinate prediction is slow and coarse: discrete tokenization breaks spatial continuity, and point regression fails on multi-contact affordances and irregular shapes. PhysMoE routes upper layers to specialized spatial and linguistic experts, encodes each coordinate as a single token using the visual encoder's positional embeddings, and adds differentiable signed-distance-field supervision for pointing. The result: a 15.7% average gain and 8.31× throughput speedup across nine grounding benchmarks, with text-based embodied reasoning preserved.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13000)

**Fei-Fei Li's Atlas keynote, one day on**

At 3 p.m. Central on November 11, Fei-Fei Li presented Atlas — an autoregressive frame-generation model unifying pixel-level image generation and 3D scene reconstruction — followed by a fireside chat with AMD CEO Lisa Su, their first joint public appearance since AMD's $8.2 billion all-stock acquisition of World Labs was announced. The pitch for robotics: generate interactive 3D environments from a few photos as synthetic training data. The honest open question, as the write-up notes: the distance from reconstructing a warehouse to a robot stably grasping arbitrary objects inside it.

**Astribot's CoRL trio: PLaW-VLA, MobiAgent, and ARMS**

Astribot announced three CoRL 2026 papers: PLaW-VLA, which models task-relevant futures in latent space for VLA policies and claims 11.8 points over π0.5 on RoboTwin 2.0's hardest long-horizon group at about one-nineteenth the latency of generative world-action models; MobiAgent, a dual-loop framework that self-improves during deployment and reports 65% on BEHAVIOR-1K long-horizon tasks; and ARMS, an always-on streaming policy with visual triggering and self-action memory.
