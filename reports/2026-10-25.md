## Papers

**Sensorimotor Alignment: A Platonic Proxy for Embodied Policy Success**

Sensorimotor Alignment (S²) asks whether a robot model's sensory representations converge toward the geometry of physical motion, as the Platonic Representation Hypothesis predicts for vision and language. The training-free metric — overlap between an episode's nearest neighbors in sensory space and in end-effector-trajectory space — correlates r = +0.717 with SimplerEnv success across 12 VLAs, beats VQA scores at picking VLM backbones before fine-tuning, and holds for world action models and real-robot policies, offering a cheap way to rank models before any closed-loop rollout.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31670) · [Project page](https://yunfeixie233.github.io/s2/) · [Code](https://github.com/yunfeixie233/sensorimotor-alignment)

**Externalise the Memory: A Pre-Registered Study of Where an Embodied Agent's Experience Should Live**

A pre-registered study asks whether an embodied agent's experience should live in its weights or outside them. Distilling a memory-augmented teacher's behavior into LoRA student weights barely helped (+1 pp, not significant), but re-attaching the teacher's frozen retrieval buffer to fixed student weights added +34 pp, matching the teacher — and the same buffer alone lifted a base model to 48%. The recipe: keep memory external and retrievable, not baked in.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30478)

**PhysibleMDM: Enforcing Inference-Time Physical Feasibility Constraints on Motion Diffusion Models via Noise Optimization**

Disney Research and MIT's PhysibleMDM makes motion-diffusion output physically feasible without retraining the generator: it optimizes the diffusion noise at inference time with a gradient-free evolution strategy against kinematic checks (ground penetration, slip, self-collision) and constrained inverse-dynamics residuals. On an SMPL humanoid it cuts ground penetration 75%, self-collision 92%, and slip 74%, giving RL trackers cleaner reference motion.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30500)

**MPBRS: Multi-Potential-Based Reward Shaping for Unified Reward Configuration in Humanoid Locomotion**

Reward tuning is the fiddliest part of humanoid locomotion RL, and naive shaping terms can fight the desired behavior or overfit one robot. MPBRS uses multi-potential-based shaping — which guides exploration without changing the optimal policy — in a single reward configuration that works across nine humanoid robots with only robot metadata swapped in, showing more consistent scaling and less tuning sensitivity than standard shaping.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30334) · [Code](https://anonymous.4open.science/r/MPBRS-5448)

## Hot Discussions

**DEEP Robotics unveils five AI advances, from world models to a real-world benchmark**

DEEP Robotics unveiled five AI advances on October 24 — a world model that predicts outcomes before acting, a VLA that turns words into motion, better policy training, memory that updates as the world changes, and a real-world benchmark built on hundreds of hours of data — alongside clips of its DR02 humanoid climbing stairs autonomously and walking rainy Hangzhou streets. **Astra's direct robot control reframes the data-collection debate**

A research note making the rounds argues Astra's direct robot control is redrawing the VLA/WAM map: as general models take over task understanding and planning, the bottleneck shifts to the physical experience only real operation provides — especially contact and tactile feedback, like knowing why a key won't turn. In that world, the scarcest asset is data covering contact, slip, jams, and failure recovery, not more generic demonstrations.

[View post](https://www.threads.com/@mingyuan0168/post/De6aUcuEgLx)

**KAIST Humanoid v0.9 spells out its own name under load**

KAIST's Humanoid v0.9 demo spells out "KAIST" with oversized letters — pushing a 35 kg "S", lifting a 20 kg "I", and walking while carrying the "T" — using only onboard visual feedback, no external cameras, with skills learned from RL plus human demonstrations. A research demo, not a product, but a compact show of whole-body control under load.
