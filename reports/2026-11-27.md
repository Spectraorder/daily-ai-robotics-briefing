## Hot Discussions

**XPENG debuts the IRON humanoid and its Turing Physical AI Architecture**

At its AI Day in Guangzhou on November 25, XPENG unveiled the Turing Physical AI Architecture — its VLA 3.0 stack spanning System 0 body control, System 1 real-time VLA 2.0 response, and System 2 vision-language planning — and debuted the IRON humanoid, with 76 degrees of freedom, over 1,500 tactile sensing points, and three in-house Turing chips delivering up to 2,250 TOPS. IRON starts training as a store assistant in Q1 2027, with mass production slated for January 2027 and a public launch in Q2 2027; XPENG also unveiled WorldGPT, a world model it says plans and predicts across six machine types, from cars to humanoids.

**ATLAS learns whole-body skills from one human video — no VLA training required**

A newly released agentic harness, ATLAS, skips the usual System 1 VLA or world-action-model training entirely: a vision-language agent (System 2) edits a single human demonstration video into motion references for a frozen whole-body tracker (System 0), with online reinforcement learning correcting the tracking gap at test time. On real-robot floor picks, online correction lifts success from 25% to 63%, and on three whole-body pick tasks it succeeds in 29, 28, and 30 of 30 episodes — competitive with policies fine-tuned on 100 robot demonstrations per task.

[View post](https://www.threads.com/@eugenewwiill/post/DgQM_DhEo3p) · [Project page](https://atlas-humanoid.github.io/blog/)

**A walkthrough of π*0.6: how Physical Intelligence's VLA learns from experience**

A new explainer video walks through RECAP, the training method behind Physical Intelligence's π*0.6: episodes are collected, a value function labels each action's advantage, and the policy trains conditioned on a "positive advantage" token — a loop of autonomous and human-corrected episodes that reportedly doubled throughput and halved failure rates on hard tasks after 13 hours. The paper itself is from November 2025, but the walkthrough is circulating today as a clear breakdown of RL post-training for VLAs.
