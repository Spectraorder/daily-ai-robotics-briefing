# Daily AI Robotics Briefing — November 11, 2026

Wednesday's edition asks uncomfortable questions about the field's favorite models: a nearest-neighbor lookup nearly matches fine-tuned VLAs on LIBERO, memory-augmented policies turn out to be reading a clock, and two new world action models try to make imagination cheaper and more interaction-aware — while CoRL week keeps delivering, with NVIDIA's GR00T 2 and an open 29B world model from MBZUAI.

## Papers

**How Much of Your VLA Is kNN**

VLAs now clear 95% on LIBERO, but how much of that is generalization? Retrieving nearby demonstrations and replaying their action chunks scores 91.7% on LIBERO with no policy training at all — and under LIBERO-Plus perturbations, retrieval degrades least of any method tested. Across nine public VLAs, fine-tuning mostly just makes the vision-language backbone a better retrieval key. A sobering baseline every VLA paper should probably report.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13160)

**LaST0.5: A Generalist World Action Model with Interaction-Centric Latent Reasoning**

World action models usually hand action generation a global scene representation — but manipulation lives in local robot–object contact. LaST0.5 inserts an interaction-centric latent between world modeling and action generation, unifying a World Expert, a Latent Expert, and an Action Expert in a mixture-of-transformers. It tops the compared baselines on RLBench and LIBERO, suggesting the representation that predicts the world is not quite the representation that should move the arm.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14048)

![The LaST0.5 framework: World, Latent, and Action experts in a mixture-of-transformers](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.14048v1/method.png)

*The LaST0.5 framework (Figure 2 from the paper).*

**Is Your Robot's Memory Just a Clock? Temporal Shortcuts in Memory-Augmented VLA Policies**

Memory-augmented VLAs sample past frames uniformly and stamp them with time steps — so a robot that merely pauses or fidgets its wrist gets counted as having made progress. On RoboMME counting tasks, a brief null event cuts success from 47 to 16 of 50 on one task and 44 to 10 on another; the policy reads elapsed time, not completed work. The authors' fix replaces time-stamped frames with event-based memory. A sharp warning for anyone trusting a memory policy to know when a job is done.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14488) · [Code](https://github.com/bennidict23/memory-clock)

**Elastic-WAM: Adaptive Spatiotemporal Imagination for Efficient Robot Control**

World action models buy foresight at the cost of iterative video denoising. Elastic-WAM stops paying a fixed price: a spatiotemporal refinement-demand field decides when to stop denoising and which future regions deserve more compute, varying the budget by task and state. It holds LIBERO and RoboCasa accuracy while cutting inference cost — the kind of efficiency work that decides whether world models run on real robots or only in papers.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14257) · [Project page](https://lihaitian.com/Elastic-WAM/)

## Hot Discussions

**Gemini Robotics 2.5, on X — and a safety-score correction**

DeepMind's launch post for Gemini Robotics 2.5, verified on X, frames the model as physical AI that "learns hundreds of everyday skills," from folding laundry to sorting car parts on a factory floor. One correction to the launch coverage: the widely quoted 99.5% jailbreak-resistance score belongs to Gemini 4 Argon, the reasoning model, not to the 2.5 action model itself.

[View post](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2120169787896525104) · [Announcement](https://deepmind.google/blog/gemini-robotics-2-5-accelerates-real-world-physical-intelligence/)

**PAN 2: an open 29B world model for physical AI**

MBZUAI's Institute of Foundation Models released PAN 2, a 29-billion-parameter diffusion transformer that takes a scene plus a text action and simulates the outcome as video, up to 20 seconds. Weights, training code, and inference code all ship under Apache 2.0 — unusually open for this category — and it leads open models on the Physics-IQ Verified leaderboard. The honest caveat, from IFM's own numbers: it still completes only about a third of multi-step plans.

[Announcement](https://ifm.ai/pan2/press-release/)

**GR00T 2 lands at CoRL — 95% after five demos, with an asterisk**

NVIDIA introduced GR00T 2 at CoRL in Austin: an open world action model on Cosmos that learns dexterous skills largely from human egocentric video, hitting 95% average success on 50 unseen tasks after five demonstrations each (40% zero-shot). Read the fine print before citing the headline: the 50 tasks were selected among those the model learns with five episodes, and the code and weights arrive in December, not today.

[Announcement](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/robotics-simulation-advancements-corl/?ncid=so-othe-843781-vt48)
