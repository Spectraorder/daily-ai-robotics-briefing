# Daily AI Robotics Briefing — November 23, 2026

Monday's edition draws on the fresh arXiv listing — five papers led by a world model that refuses to walk through walls and a policy built straight from an image generator — plus X Square Robot's WALL-OSS-1.0 launch, two world-action-model preprints making the rounds on social, and a hard-tech podcast on why today's robots still aren't built for AI.

## Papers

**GenPA: Image Generators as Efficient Robot Policies**

Most VLA work treats future visual prediction as the bridge between generative pretraining and control; GenPA argues the image generator itself is the policy. It fine-tunes a generation backbone with a lightweight 19M-parameter action adapter and reuses the native generative objective for perception co-training, needing only a single forward pass at deployment — no visual generation, no iterative action denoising. It hits 99.10% on LIBERO and 83.79% on LIBERO-Plus, at 20.9 ms per action chunk on an H100, 1.7–3.4× faster than strong VLA and world-action-model baselines.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27446)

**Non-penetrating World Models**

Interactive video world models happily walk through walls — none of the six open-source models tested could reliably stop at a physical obstacle. NoPeWorld fixes this with a small prediction head on a frozen diffusion transformer's hidden states that reads out whether a candidate action would penetrate an obstacle, swapping unsafe actions for safe ones that best preserve intent. It cuts the physical violation rate from 74% to 16% averaged across six world models, and generalizes zero-shot to open-domain images, third-person views, and moving obstacles.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27963) · [Project page](https://nopeworld.github.io)

![The NoPeWorld framework: candidate motions are labelled against true geometry, a head predicts the labels from frozen DiT states, and unsafe actions are replaced at inference](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.27963v1/fig_arch.png)

*The NoPeWorld framework (Figure 2 from the paper).*

**Ego4Gen: Generating Geometry-Grounded Motion Tokens as a World-Action Interface**

Ego4Gen is a world model for 4D future generation in manipulation: from a single egocentric image and a text prompt it jointly generates RGB frames, dense point maps, and dense scene flow, explicitly predicting how appearance, geometry, and motion evolve. Built on Cosmos3-Nano with modality-routed LoRA, its predicted geometry–motion latents can be decoded into 3D structure or mapped to robot actions via a lightweight inverse-dynamics network, FlowInverse — and they beat latent-action and video-latent baselines out of distribution, with closed-loop policy evaluations showing the 4D futures translate into real control.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27513)

![Framework of Ego4Gen: built on the image-to-video pathway of Cosmos3-Nano, augmented with geometry and motion modalities via modality-routed LoRA](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.27513v1/fig2.png)

*Framework of Ego4Gen (Figure 2 from the paper).*

**Embodied-Harness: Towards Recursive Self-Improvement for Robotic Manipulation**

VLM agents for manipulation usually fail the same way twice: they don't organize memory, can't attribute failures, and don't reuse experience. Embodied-Harness improves the harness, not the model weights — an Actor executes while a Reflector turns execution evidence into reusable operational guidance, backed by a hierarchical memory of short-term context and long-term experience. It reaches 82.4% on RoboTwin 2.0, 76.7% on RoboSPA, and 94.4% on RMBench, plus 96.7% average success across three real-world manipulation tasks.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27689)

![Framework of Embodied-Harness: the Actor executes through a perception–action loop while hierarchical memory maintains short-term context and long-term experience](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.27689v1/Embodied-Harness.png)

*Framework of Embodied-Harness (Figure 1 from the paper).*

**SOAR: Training-Free Rank-Consistent Rollout Compression for Latent World Model Planning**

Latent world models plan by rolling out hundreds of candidate action sequences, so one decision can take tens of seconds — but a sampling-based planner only needs the ranking of candidates, not their exact costs. SOAR exploits that: at each decision it probes a few candidates under several token budgets and keeps the smallest budget that preserves their ranking, compressing as far as the current decision allows, with no training and no task labels. Across seven navigation and manipulation tasks it matches dense planning success at 2.3× speedup and retains 98% at 2.5×.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27639) · [Code](https://github.com/starrywiki/SOAR)

## Hot Discussions

**WALL-OSS-1.0 launches with 55.1% zero-shot across 312 tasks**

X Square Robot unveiled WALL-OSS-1.0 today, a VLA foundation model built on Qwen3.5 with "native VLA pretraining" — vision, language, and action trained jointly from the start rather than bolting actions onto a pretrained VLM. In an open-ended real-robot evaluation it succeeded on 55.1% of 312 evaluator-chosen tasks with no task-specific demonstrations, alongside the XR Harness agentic framework and Lite (4B) and Nano (2B) variants; code and weights are promised open-source, with no date yet. (All benchmark figures are the company's own, with no independent reproduction yet.)

**WEVO: post-training humanoid VLAs inside a video world model**

A November 23 reel breaks down WEVO (arXiv:2611.25251, November 18), a teleoperation-free framework for post-training a whole-body humanoid VLA inside an action-conditioned video world model — RL without the unsafe real-world exploration or the sim-to-real exploitation of simulator RL, by imagining the rollouts instead.

[View post](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DgEmeMxFDBW/) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25251)

**ResWAM: learn what changes, not what stays**

A November 23 reel explains ResWAM (arXiv:2611.19559, November 14): world-action models waste capacity supervising complete future observations, most of which barely change over an action chunk. ResWAM supervises only the residual — what actually changes — to make world-action-model training sharper about interaction dynamics.

[View post](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DgFNhMqB0N_/) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19559)
