# Daily AI Robotics Briefing — November 9, 2026

Monday's edition challenges the VLA orthodoxy from two directions: Apple's AMP1 shows a generalist frontier model driving a manipulator directly, beating leading VLAs and world-action models zero-shot, and a CMU position paper argues computer-use agents are embodied generalists too — plus Meituan's industrial-scale VLA, a chunk-free action head, and a tactile world-action model, as CoRL week gets underway in Austin.

## Papers

**On Agentic Manipulation Policies**

Apple's team asks whether manipulation still needs specialist policies at all. AMP1 puts a generalist frontier model directly in the control loop — it observes, writes and runs code, and revises its actions as the task unfolds — with no demonstrations or task-specific training. It reaches 85.6% zero-shot on RoboLab-120, against 49.2% for the leading VLA and 42.9% for the leading world-action model, and succeeds in 104 of 108 real-world trials across twelve tasks and three robots, inventing strategies like using a scale as a measuring instrument; adding a persistent memory store cuts the RoboDojo bimanual failure rate 4.6×.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09892)

**Computer-Use Agents Are Embodied Generalists**

CMU's Pranjal Aggarwal and Sean Welleck argue embodied tasks are just another software interface: render the environment on a screen, give the agent keyboard and mouse controls, and a computer-use agent can do the job with the same screenshots-and-clicks skills it uses on desktop software. One unchanged agent design, with only the software swapped, took first, second, third, and sixth place across four ECCV workshop competitions spanning navigation, dialogue-driven navigation, 3D segmentation, and video segmentation — evidence, they argue, that embodied AI can converge on a single general model the way computer use already has.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09847) · [Project page](https://pranjal2041.github.io/cua-embodied)

![Overview: computer-use agents operate software through keyboard and mouse; physical environments are no different once exposed through software](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.09847v1/teaser.png)

**Meituan-Robotics-0: A Vision-Language-Action Foundation Model for Desktop Manipulation**

Meituan's robotics team ships an industrial-scale VLA: 147 million vision-language samples plus 15,060 hours of robot trajectories across 12 embodiments, trained in three stages from discrete action tokens to flow-matching continuous control, then real-world post-training. A "Rollback" mechanism pairs replaced policy segments with human corrections for correction-based training, and an event-driven agent keeps persistent task memory so the system can suspend and resume tasks when conditions change. MR0 hits 93.6% on RoboTwin 2.0 and beats π0.5 under matched post-training on three real-robot tasks.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08577) · [Project page](https://meituan-robotics.github.io/Meituan-Robotics-0/) · [Code](https://github.com/Meituan-Robotics/Meituan-Robotics-0)

**ODEAction: From Predicting Chunks to Evolving Actions**

VLAs usually predict actions in chunks, which creates jerky discontinuities where one chunk ends and the next begins. ODEAction replaces chunk prediction with continuous action evolution: it learns a transition field over action space and integrates it with an ordinary differential equation, generating each next action from the current one with explicit dependencies. Implemented as a drop-in action head for any VLM backbone, it improves both success rates and trajectory smoothness on LIBERO, EBench, and RoboTwin, with the largest gains on fine manipulation, and transfers to real robots.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09672)

![From action-chunk prediction to continuous action evolution: ODEAction integrates a learned transition field instead of predicting fixed chunks](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.09672v1/figure1.png)

**MDF++: Visuo-Tactile World Action Model with End-to-end Representation Learning**

World-action models usually build on frozen encoders trained for reconstruction, which learn features that look good but aren't tuned for control. MDF++ trains its vision, tactile, force, and proprioception encoders end-to-end with a latent diffusion world-action model, using a multi-task strategy that exploits cross-modal correlations to avoid feature collapse. On contact-rich tasks including real-world bolt screwing and nut mating, it outperforms world-action models built on frozen pretrained representations by up to 50%.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09293) · [Project page](https://jointmdf.github.io/)

## Hot Discussions

**Mabel: a fully open-source mobile manipulator for about $8,000 in parts**

A November 7 reel introduces Mabel, an anthropomorphic mobile manipulator built in a few months inside a small apartment by researchers from NYU and the University of Toronto: 56 degrees of freedom, two 7-DoF arms, two 17-DoF hands, a lifting torso, and a holonomic base — teleoperated end-to-end with an Apple Vision Pro and no handheld controllers. Hardware, firmware, controllers, MuJoCo simulation, and the imitation-learning stack are all open source.

[View post](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dfb2hCPAjIZ/)

**EKA's Vision-Force-Action model picks at 1,500 per hour**

A November 9 demo reel shows EKA's robotic arm running its Vision-Force-Action (VFA) model at 1,500 picks per hour, filmed at 1× speed — rapidly identifying, grasping, and moving small objects including pen caps and bottle caps as a person keeps placing them on the table.

[View post](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfhOk-Jndhi/)

**SymSkill learns robot skills from minutes of play data**

A November 7 explainer walks through SymSkill from UPenn's GRASP Lab, an ICRA 2026 Best Paper in Planning & Control: instead of massive datasets, it learns from minutes of unstructured play, segments motion into skills by tracking gripper speed, uses a VLM to build symbolic predicates like "lid on pan," and chains the learned operators with classical A* planning — recovering mid-task when a human interrupts.

[View post](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dfc8aNsvDr2/)
